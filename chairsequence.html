Prompt 04 · Futuristic Layers

A floating white egg chair centered in a clean arcade room with warm ambient lighting and a cozy nighttime atmosphere. Anime-style posters glow softly from the walls while rhythm game cabinets and a large slider game occupy the background. Delicate futuristic light patterns inspired by Chiho Aoshima drift across the walls and ceiling, creating a dreamlike digital atmosphere.

A floating white egg chair centered in a warm arcade room illuminated by soft amber lighting. A retro game loading bar stretches across the bottom of the composition while the cozy arcade atmosphere remains unchanged.

Prompt 06 · Final Environment

A floating white egg chair centered in a clean arcade room with warm amber lighting. All characters have been removed while posters, rhythm game cabinets, and small decorative objects remain. Purple neon has been toned down in favor of warm white and muted orange illumination. Subtle flowing stripe motifs and a pixel-art loading bar create a nostalgic, dreamlike futuristic atmosphere.
