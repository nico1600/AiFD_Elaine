FIG 01.0 // ZOZI LOGOMARK
Primary Iridescent Emblem

VISUAL ANATOMY & USAGE

The ZOZI mark merges a sharp geometric 5-point star silhouette with a holographic compact disc core. The metallic silver chrome accents contrast against soft dreamy blues and pinks, representing the tactile nostalgia of early-2000s media and surreal worldbuilding.

✓ Clear Space

Maintain a minimum padding of 25% of the star's outer diameter around all sides.

✓ Surface Contrast

Best presented on deep ultraviolet surfaces (#161224) or off-white archival cards (#FAF7EE).
