ARTIST STATEMENT

This mission patch for Baruch College brings together space science and the fun energy of vintage arcade games. Right in the center, a glowing experimental capsule floats against a grid-lined globe, representing Baruch's real-world involvement in microgravity research aboard the International Space Station. Orbiting around it, pixelated aliens add a playful touch of nostalgic gaming aesthetic, turning this high-level scientific exploration into an adventurous playground. These elements seek to evoke the excitement of childhood imagination with the possibilities of modern scientific discovery. In addition, the energetic typography combines futuristic lettering and arcade-inspired gradients. By blasting “Baruch” into space for SSEP 2027, this mission patch anchors our college spirit straight to the cosmos. The contrast between scientific imagery and playful digital elements makes complex research feel more accessible and engaging. Ultimately, this design celebrates how Baruch’s spirit of curiosity and innovation powers our everyday exploration beyond the classroom into the infinite expanse of space.
